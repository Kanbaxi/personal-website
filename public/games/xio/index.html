Xio

Game Overview & Rules

  • Turn-Based Timer: You have 5 seconds to act. The turn resolves instantly when you input.
  • Energy (0E): Press Space to Xio (+1 Energy, limitless!). Leaves you vulnerable!
  • Attacks: J Jab (1E | 0.5 DMG)
    K Crush (2E | 1.0 DMG)
    L Slam (3E | 1.5 DMG)
  • Defenses (0E): S Low Guard blocks Jab
    W High Guard blocks Crush
    J Jab interrupts & blocks Slam
  • Timer runs out = Default to Low Guard!

Select Your Role

Peasant: 10 HP. You and an AI partner fight the Boss.
Boss: 50 HP. You fight two AI Peasants. (Attacks auto-target healthiest Peasant).
